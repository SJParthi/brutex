# hunt-indicators — crates/indicators + crates/vocab at 1087e54

**Verdict.** The core arithmetic holds up. I checked these against their standard definitions and against exact arithmetic: the classic pivot / CPR ladder, the R4/R5/S4/S5 extension, the previous-day, five-session, current-day and gap Fibonacci ladders, the EMA α=2/(n+1), Wilder's 1/n ATR smoothing, the volume-weighted population sigma for VWAP, and the weekday mapping. A probe of 9,444 VWAP bars matched an exact rational reference with 0 mismatches. The state resets are clock-based (IST day, 3-minute bucket, ORB minute windows), so a missing minute no longer leaks a neighbour bar. The overflow and division-by-zero guards are in place. Over 26,471 evaluated rows, truth ⊆ known held on every row and no non-live bit was emitted. The docs/03 ↔ table name, status and tombstone checks are green (`vocab --test table` 19/19). Most of the prior pass's indicator findings are **fixed at HEAD**: VWAP floor-first sigma, the anchored `check` → `check_evaluable` change, the refused-bar cursor walk, the overlay clamp read from later days, the 3-bars-vs-3-minutes gap candle, and the ORB/GapFib per-bit overlay cost. The new defects are semantic:
- **(1) The warm-up gate opens while the seed still dominates.** EMA200 and ATR10 seed from one bar, and "warm" opens after `period` bars. At that point the seed still carries about 13.8% (EMA200) or 38.7% (ATR10) of the weight. The probe shows `close_below_ema200` set where an SMA-seeded EMA200 says the close is above.
- **(2) Two pairs of opposite patterns fire on one bar.** Bullish engulfing and bullish harami (157/159) fire together on equal-body reversals, and so do the bearish pair (158/160). This happened on 43 of 26,471 random rows.
- **(3) Two patterns contradict the table's own description and the standard pattern.** `pat_homing_pigeon` (228) and `pat_identical_three_crows` (230) are implemented as different shapes from what their table rows and the standard definitions describe.
- **(4) Doc-stale and still-open items.**

No high-severity finding.

Probe files `crates/indicators/tests/zz_audit_hunt-indicators_{1,2}.rs` were run and deleted. `git status --porcelain` shows nothing of mine.
