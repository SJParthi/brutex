## Read but not probed (and why)

- The engine checkpoint `validate_for` checks the bar count only. Its docs say full column-byte identity is the caller's job (resume.rs). Not reported.
- `runner::significance::p_value` uses A&S 7.1.26, with an absolute error of about 1.5e-7, which is too coarse for Bonferroni/BH thresholds near 1e-10. It has no production caller (grep shows only tests), and `bonferroni_t` uses the Acklam quantile instead. Not reported.
- `pbo::place` rounds a half-rank by integer division. This is documented as a lossy adapter (pbo.rs:402-405).
