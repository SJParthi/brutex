That test drives 260 bars stamped 00:00 IST through the production `Evaluator` and `Column::build` with VWAP availability both Present and Absent. **What remains open ("unprovable in this crate", narrowed):**
- The test proves V-04 only for daily bars stamped at IST midnight. That premise is not enforced:
  - The store admits a daily bar at any whole second. `crates/store/src/file.rs:705-707` says vendors "stamp a daily bar at midnight, the open or the close" (D-0915), and `crates/pull/src/session.rs:29` says the same.
  - The cited pull test `the_daily_rung_is_anchored_at_ist_midnight_and_not_at_the_open` (`crates/pull/tests/anchor.rs:120`) pins only daily bars that `pull::fold` builds, not vendor daily endpoints.
  - A daily bar stamped at 09:15 falls inside `early_morning` (bit 44, `docs/03-vocabulary.md:172`).
- The daily rung is refused as a swept rung: `cli::swept_rung("1day")` is pinned by `cli::tests::operator_text_names_only_rungs_and_commands_this_binary_accepts` (`lib.rs:23900`). But `cli verify` DOES fold the stored 1day series through `evaluator_stored` and `Sweeper::run` (`lib.rs:8489`, `8539-8543`). So a daily column is built in production, and V-04's truth there depends on the vendor's daily stamp. **Which vendors stamp at the open: UNVERIFIED.** No charter or vendor-sample entry found.
