**G2-5 · medium · cost/robustness: a RECORDED tier walk captures every judged tier (consequence of D-1731/D-1734; not in the D-1734 bound) — runtime UNVERIFIED (traced, not executed)**
- **Where:** crates/cli/src/lib.rs:13788-13870 (`tier_rows`), called per tier from `walk_tiers` (13155-13164); candidate_trades.rs:348-399 (`Capture::tier`) and 444-526 (`record_inner`).

  - `audit_bars` starts a capture for every recorded run with an attempt (lib.rs:22167-22180) and passes it as `Pricing.capture` into `trade_and_screen`, then `screen_cascade`, then `walk_tiers`.

  - The cumulative acknowledgement budget is 64 MiB (candidate_trades.rs:61, 376-377). That is about 10,000 tiers at C=98, about 1,760 at C=577 (the operator's logged run) and about 100 at the default `screen_cap()` of 10,000.

  - D-1734 says the capture "still records one tier per policy judged" but gives no cost; the 06-limits.md:15514 bound omits it.
- **Fix:** Capture only the tiers whose answer is used. That is the operator's own policy screen, plus the MET tier, or the last tier on `NoneAdmit`. Concretely:
  - Pass `capture: None` into `tier_rows` inside `judge`.
  - Re-run `tier_rows` with the real capture only for the tier that `finish_screen` is called for. It is O(C × 2 × K) on the cached grids, so it is cheap.
  - This changes capture tier ordinals and catalog shape, so it needs a D-entry and a capture-format note.
