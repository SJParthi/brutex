What does “R” mean?

R is the maximum single-trade loss selected for a run, expressed in price units. Backtest shows it when configured. Price limits scale from that amount using checked integer arithmetic.

Example only: R = 50 price points
10R = 500 points. −R = −50 points. The example does not set the campaign's risk amount or account capital.

Two settings outside the profile

  • Worst single-trade loss must be no more than the configured R.
  • The inherited reward/risk default requires the smallest positive pessimistic trade to be at least three times the largest losing trade. An explicit override can change the threshold; Backtest shows its effective value.
  • No observed loss leaves that ratio unmeasured. This guide does not supply a live policy value.
  • The index workflow separately requires at least 60% winning eligible days and its weekly and loss-streak rules. The table's winning-trade share does not replace them.
  • The 15:10 IST execution deadline is a separate mandatory rule.
  • Excluded trading costs remain excluded and must remain labelled.

Four outcomes that must stay different

PassedMeasured evidence met the check. This describes the tested history.
FailedThe evidence was measured and missed the configured limit.
UnmeasuredRequired evidence was unavailable or not calculated. It is not a pass.
RefusedInvalid data, a broken contract or a resource limit prevented a valid assessment.
