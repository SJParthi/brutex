## 1. What was built

All new code is in new modules of `crates/costs`. The existing options API
(`trip`, `regime`, `rate`, `venue`, `money`, `fill`, `lot`, …) is unchanged, and
`regime.rs`, `rate.rs` and `venue.rs` were **not edited** (CHG-0 owns
`regime.rs`). No dependency was added; the crate graph is unchanged.

| Module | Owns |
|---|---|
| `exact` | `Ratio`, an exact integer rate (numerator / denominator, crate-private constructor); `round_half_up` (fallible) and `half_up::<D>` (compile-time divisor, infallible); `Rupees`, the ₹ renderer |
| `charge_error` | `ChargeError`, every refusal of the stack, each with its own words |

### Why the two modes differ

Two rules account for every difference between the modes (D-5112, D-5113,
`the_precise_and_calculator_modes_differ_where_the_rules_differ_and_agree_where_they_do_not`):

- **STT and stamp duty.** The calculator taxes the sell value and adds STT and
  stamp duty to the total unrounded. The precise stack taxes the day's VWAP
  value and rounds each to the rupee per client-security-day.
- **GST.** The calculator's GST base leaves out the SEBI fee; the precise base
  includes it.

The two buy-only **intraday** readings are refused in precise mode
(`OpenIntradayPosition`). A contract note never shows a lone intraday buy,
because the position is squared off the same day.

**Three things these figures show, stated as limits.**

1. **The widest day costs about 1.26× a normal one, every time.** Once a rate
   product passes 2⁶⁴, `i128` division leaves the 64-bit fast path for the
   compiler's software routine. That routine's cost is bounded by the 128-bit
   width and not by the magnitude, so the cost is still constant, but it is a
   different constant. It is printed and not gated; recorded in D-5118 and
   `docs/06-limits.md` §CHG-1.
2. **One run showed C-CHG-03 at 1.651×**, the huge day's median at 333.8 ns.
   It did not reproduce in the next four runs, which all came in at 1.00×.
   It is recorded here rather than dropped. It is under the 3.0× ceiling.
3. **The max column is the container's scheduler, not the stack**, and it
   reached 0.5 ms in one batch. Neither `add` nor `settle` loops over orders,
   allocates or takes a lock. The p99 column is the figure to read; on these
   runs it is at most 488.3 ns per client-security-day (the widest) and 40.7
   ns per order. Absolute figures
   belong to this machine; only the ratios are gated.

## 5. What is UNVERIFIED

Each item is recorded in `docs/06-limits.md` §CHG-1, and in
`docs/00-charter.md` for the rates.

1. **No source was fetched by this session.** The environment's network
   policy refused `nsearchives.nseindia.com` and `dhan.co` (403 on CONNECT).
   Every figure and circular number comes from the operator's brief, no
   circular body was read, and no digest is recorded.
2. **Four rates are priced while UNVERIFIED** and are listed on every output
   that uses them:
   - BSE cash ₹375 per crore all-in;
   - BSE options' IPFT, taken as zero;
   - equity delivery and MTF STT 0.1%;
   - futures STT 0.05%.
3. **Rounding of an exact half.** Half-up is a choice. Of the five vectors,
   none lands on an exact half, so they do not settle it.
4. **Aggregation level.** The exchange charge, SEBI fee and GST are
   aggregated per client-security-day. A contract note may total them per
   note instead.
5. **GST basis nuances.** Whether SEBI is in the base is exactly where the two
   modes differ; DP and the square-off fee carry their own 18%.
6. **Dhan's calculator total.** The last-paisa rule is unknown (§3).
7. **Effective dates.** None was seen in a source. The dated selector
   therefore refuses equity and futures on every date. Applying today's rates
   to a 2018 trade is the operator's labelled choice, not an accuracy claim.
8. **Ceiling square-off fee on F&O.** Applied when the caller marks the exit;
   its F&O applicability and cut-off time are UNVERIFIED.
9. **Contract notes.** **Full precision needs the operator's real Dhan
   contract notes.** No output has been compared with one.
