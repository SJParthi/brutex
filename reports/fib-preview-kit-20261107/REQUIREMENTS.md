Every result step and every independent check verifies each item; a missing item is BLOCKING.
1. THE BAR (raised by the operator Fri 6 Nov ~12:3x IST: "per month I need 2.5 lakhs"): every UNSEEN walk-forward month nets >= Rs 2,50,000 after every charge (12.5% of the Rs 20 lakh account, or 12.5% of a grown month-start capital if higher); a no-trade month fails; the earlier +2.5% (Rs 50,000) tier is reported beside it.
2. Ranking among passers: lowest max drawdown first, then net profit, entry accuracy, fewer trades, shorter holding, higher net per trade.
3. Walk-forward always (choices on earlier months only) and a luck test counting every option tried; no look-ahead anywhere.
4. F&O stocks only, cash, intraday (square-off by 15:10), Rs 20 lakh margin and Rs 1 crore exposure in total; fills at the worst traded price of the GDFL second; every charge of the cost table.
5. PSRB: stop loss and exit ALWAYS the Supertrend (operator-inputs item 19).
6. 7. 8. 9. Honesty: never a fake pass; PREVIEW labels until the full engine, data fixes, live replay and paper trading confirm; nothing reaches real money without the operator.
10. Never stop until the bar is met on unseen months, or until exactly what blocks it is shown.
11. NOTHING FIXED (memory nothing-fixed-everything-generated): except the pass bar and exchange/broker facts, every number is generated from each stock's own past percentiles inside the training months, coarse to fine, inside the walk-forward, and labelled 'generated' in results.
