## How this was done, and what each kind of claim rests on

* **Read.** Eight read-only tracing passes covered the 89 routes registered in
  `crates/api/src/server.rs:15995-16395` (`route_table`), the per-request
  middleware in `router_serving` (`server.rs:15501` onward), and the hot paths
  named in the task. Every `file:line` below was read in the source by a pass;
  the lines marked **✓** were re-read by me before they went in this table.
  **Every line number is on the base, c9e3472**, which this branch leaves
  byte-identical in every file it does not edit. Rows F1-F4 cite the fixed code
  on this branch. In the four edited files, base lines after the edits sit
  lower on the branch (from `git diff -U0 c9e3472`): `bars_route.rs` +5 after
  base 94 and +60 after base 1231 (base 629 is branch 634); `facets.rs` +5
  after 197, +10 after 207, +25 after 310, +21 after 1288, +13 after 1361;
  `gdfl_nfo.rs` +4 after 1318, +19 after 1329, +16 after 1343; `livejson.rs`
  +16 after 165.
* **Measured.** Only the rows whose "measured" column carries numbers. Every
  other row is a bound read from the code, **UNVERIFIED as a latency**.
  `CLAUDE.md` §3 rule 6.
* **Profile and host.** Every number here was taken on this cloud container:
  Intel Xeon @ 2.80 GHz, 4 cores, 15 GB, Linux, rustc 1.97.1. The new
  `store` and `pull` rows were run twice, under `cargo bench --profile dev`
  (opt-level 3, no LTO) and then under the real bench profile
  (`[profile.bench]`: fat LTO, `codegen-units = 1`), and both are quoted,
  marked *dev* and *bench*. The `api` rows are dev-profile only: the `api`
  bench links `cli`, and compiling `cli` under the bench profile
  (`-C opt-level=3 -C linker-plugin-lto -C codegen-units=1 -C debuginfo=2`)
  was killed by the kernel (signal 9, out of memory) twice on this 15 GB host,
  once beside a test build and once with nothing else running. The ratios are
  the shape the gate asserts; the absolute numbers are this host's, not the
  operator's M4 Pro.
* **Grammar of the table.** *What grows*: E = census entries (all vendors),
  E_v = one vendor's, U = merged universe keys (about 2,780), M = months in a
  request's range, n = bars, R = run records of the pull journal, H = saved
  checkpoints, J = import-journal day rows, P = epoch files in one census
  segment directory. *Named?* is the `docs/06-limits.md` line of the paragraph
  naming that exact cost, or **NO**. *Severity*: **HIGH** = grows per request on
  a polled route, or per row / per bar, and is unbounded or unnamed; **MED** =
  bounded by a large cap, or named but unmeasured; **LOW** = named, bounded,
  and either measured or fixed-small.
